There's a ball of iron under your feet as hot as the surface of the Sun. 🌍🔥

In 2013, researchers in Grenoble squeezed iron between two diamonds and found Earth's centre is about 6,000 °C (give or take 500). The Sun's surface is about 5,500 °C.

The pressure down there, over 3 million atmospheres, keeps that iron solid. The heat stirs the liquid iron around it, and that stirring makes the magnetic field your compass follows.

Which surprises you more: the heat or the pressure? 👇

Sources: ESRF (Anzellini et al., Science 2013), NASA Sun facts.
