On Mars, snowflakes may be cubes. 🧊

Earth's have six sides because of how water molecules line up. Mars also has dry-ice snow, and its fourfold symmetry means scientists expect tiny cubes. As of 2022, nobody had pictured it falling.

On Enceladus, jets spray ice and most falls back like snow.
