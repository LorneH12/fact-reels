Most neon colors contain no neon at all. 💡

Neon gas glows reddish-orange. Electricity pushes electrons through the gas, they hit atoms, and the atoms give the energy back as light.

Blue comes from argon with a little mercury, often with a coating too. Many other colors come from powder inside the glass that turns ultraviolet into visible light.
