Eight years to reach Mercury. 🛰️

The Sun's gravity keeps pulling a spacecraft faster, and go too fast and you can't stop. So BepiColombo swung past Earth once, Venus twice and Mercury six times before entering orbit on November 21, 2026.

A software glitch ended its engine burn early, leaving a 75-hour orbit instead of 58. ESA says no extra burns are needed. The two orbiters are due to separate December 9–10.

Would you have waited eight years? 👇

Sources: ESA; WRAL (NASA Ambassador Tony Rice).
