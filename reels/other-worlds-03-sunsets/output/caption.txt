On Mars, the sunset is blue. 🔵

Earth's air scatters blue away, so red gets through. Martian dust is fine enough to let blue through best and keep it near the Sun. Curiosity saw it in color on April 15, 2015.

Uranus here is a NASA simulation. The Moon's sky just stays black.
