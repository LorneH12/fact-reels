You've never seen the Sun as it is right now. ☀️

Its light takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach you, so you always see it a little in the past. The farther you look, the older the light.

The Andromeda galaxy is visible to the naked eye from a dark site, and its light left about 2.5 million years ago. Our species has been around for roughly 300,000 years.

Would you rather see the Sun live or Andromeda as it is today? 👇

Sources: Smithsonian (How Things Fly), NASA Hubble (M31), Smithsonian Human Origins.
