INSTAGRAM CAPTION

On the Moon, the sky is black. Even at noon. 🌑

Earth's air is the difference. Sunlight holds every color, and air molecules scatter short wavelengths most. Violet (400 nm) scatters about ten times more than deep red (700 nm).

So why isn't the sky violet? Sunlight has less of it, and our eyes are less sensitive to it.

Did you expect violet to win? 👇

Sources: NASA; Royal Museums Greenwich; Physics FAQ.
