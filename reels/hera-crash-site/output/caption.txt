We hit an asteroid. Now we've gone back to look. ☄️

On September 26, 2022, NASA's DART hit Dimorphos on purpose and shortened its orbit by 33 minutes. Today ESA's Hera showed what happened: one face flattened into a stadium-sized crater, a wild tumble, and hundreds of boulders flying around the pair. The rest looks unchanged.

ESA calls these first images the beginning. Hera moves in to about 13 km in December.

Which result surprised you? 👇

Source: ESA, November 25, 2026.
