Warm water in one place, rain far away. 🌧️

In El Niño, Pacific trade winds weaken and warm water moves back toward the Americas. The jet stream shifts south. The Gulf Coast and Southeast tend wetter, and the northern U.S. tends warmer and drier.

Forecasters put the chance of strong to very strong El Niño lasting through January–March 2027 at roughly 80% (November 19 update). That's odds, not a promise.
