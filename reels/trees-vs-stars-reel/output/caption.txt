Earth has more trees than the Milky Way has stars. 🌲✨

A global study in Nature estimated about 3.04 trillion trees. Our galaxy holds somewhere between 100 and 400 billion stars, so there are at least seven trees for every star.

And that count only includes trunks at least 10 cm wide.

Did you think it would be the other way round? 👇
