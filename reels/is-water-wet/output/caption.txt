Is water wet? 💧

In science, wetting is what a liquid does to a surface. By that rule, water makes other things wet.

Dictionaries split. Dictionary.com says wet means moistened, covered or soaked. Wiktionary says it can mean made up of liquid, and by that rule water is wet.

Our verdict: wet is what happens to the dry thing. Team Wet or Team Not Wet? 👇

Sources: USGS; Wikipedia (Wetting); Dictionary.com; Wiktionary.
