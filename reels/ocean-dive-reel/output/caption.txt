Mount Everest would disappear underwater in the deepest part of the ocean. 🌊

Sunlight fades by 200 m and is gone by 1,000 m. Most animals down there make their own light. Every night, trillions of them rise to feed: the biggest migration on Earth. The Titanic lies at 3,800 m; past 6,000 m is the hadal zone, named after Hades. The deepest fish ever filmed, a snailfish, was seen at 8,336 m in 2022. Researchers think that's near the limit for fish. Challenger Deep bottoms out around 10,935 m, at over 1,000 atmospheres. Two explorers first reached it in 1960 in the Trieste. Everest (8,849 m) would sit about 2 km under the surface.

Even there, a camera found a plastic bag (10,898 m). And less than a third of the seafloor has been mapped in detail.
