One deep-sea rock, 245 animals. 🪨

Scientists examined 244 fist-sized nodules from the Pacific seafloor, 4 to 6 km down, and found 4,139 animals. The average was 17 per rock. One held 245.

The rocks grow a few millimeters in a million years, and they're rich in metal.

Mine them or leave them? 👇

Source: Scientific Reports (2026), via MARUM and ScienceDaily.
