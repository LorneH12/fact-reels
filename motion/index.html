I01

A-vs-B magnitude bars

approved
“There are more trees on Earth than stars in our galaxy.”trees-vs-stars-reel

Why nothing we have works: Nothing in the library compares two huge numbers. Two bars grow to scale (3 trillion vs 100–400 billion, with the range shown as a faded band), and the winner label lands on the beat.

Reuse: Any 'more X than Y' fact; GTA map sizes (Vice City vs Los Santos).

Reuse: Any 'N in M' or percentage fact; poll results; 'only 1 in 10 players…'.

I03

Fact-or-fake stamp

approved
“Follow for more facts that sound fake, but aren't.”every fact reel (CTA)

Why nothing we have works: Our sign-off line is spoken over nothing. A FAKE? stamp flips to a REAL stamp with the source name, which turns the brand promise into a picture and a repeatable end card.

Reuse: Fact-reels end card; Wanted Level rumour checks (REAL / NOT CONFIRMED / FAKE) with 2–3 short caveats.

Pick: reply "I03 A", "I03 B", "I03 C" or "I03 all".

I04

Face-off thermometer

approved
“Researchers in France measured about six thousand degrees … The Sun's surface is about five and a half thousand.”earth-core-reel

Why nothing we have works: Two temperatures are said 4 seconds apart and the viewer has to hold the first in their head. One gauge with two needles (core 6,000 °C, Sun 5,500 °C) shows the gap at once.

Reuse: Any two readings on one scale: speed, depth, price, time.

Pick: reply "I04 A", "I04 B", "I04 C" or "I04 all".

I05

approved
“Its light takes about eight minutes and twenty seconds to reach your eyes.”old-light-reel

Why nothing we have works: A delay is hard to feel from a number. A line from 'left the Sun' to 'reached you' with a clock running 8:20, then the same line zoomed out to 2.5 million years for Andromeda.

Reuse: Travel times, delays, 'how long ago' facts; GTA in-game vs real time.

Pick: reply "I05 A", "I05 B", "I05 C" or "I05 all".
